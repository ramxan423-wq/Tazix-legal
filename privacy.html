Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

TAZIX is a task-based alarm application. This page describes how the app uses device features and permissions.

Permissions and device information

  • Notifications: to schedule and deliver alarms.
  • Camera: for optional QR/barcode and object-based challenges.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: for optional voice challenges.
  • Motion and activity: for optional movement-based challenges.
  • Alarm preferences and progress: to maintain your routines and statistics.

Permissions can be managed in iOS Settings. Some features may be unavailable if permissions are denied.

Subscriptions

If subscriptions are offered, purchases and subscription management are processed through Apple's App Store.

Data practices

Data collection, transmission, retention, and third-party SDK practices must be verified against the production version of TAZIX. This policy will be updated to accurately reflect those practices before release.

Updates

We may update this policy when the app changes.
