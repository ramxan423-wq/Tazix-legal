TAZIX Support

Help with alarms, wake-up challenges, permissions and subscriptions.

How do I create an alarm?

Open the Alarm tab, select a time and wake-up task, and save your alarm.

Why do tasks ask for permissions?

Camera, microphone, motion and notification permissions are required for certain optional features. Manage them in iOS Settings.

What if an alarm does not sound?

Check notification permissions, volume, Focus settings, and device restrictions. Test alarms before relying on them.

How do I manage a subscription?

Open iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, and choose TAZIX if listed.
